90% of an electrician's work is unseen

Turning the light on is the easy part. What matters is what's inside the wall, and that's where Blue Star spends its attention.

  • Careful drilling. Holes are placed to protect the structural beams in your house.
  • Screw terminations. Outlets are wired with screws, not quick-connects, which lowers the fire risk.
  • Laser-aligned boxes. Electrical boxes are set straight and consistent.
  • Safe wire fishing. When it's safer to open a wall than risk hidden wires, we cut and patch the drywall.
  • Sealed exterior holes. Openings are closed against water and rodents.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does an older home need an electrician, not a handyman?

Wiring materials and grounding have changed a lot since electric lighting first arrived, and mixing modern and vintage systems without the right training creates additional hazards. A licensed electrician is trained for exactly that.

How do I get an estimate?

Call 404.234.6777 or use the form below. We look at the job in person and give you a detailed estimate before any work begins.

Who does the work?

Licensed electricians supervise the work on site. Owner Gary Piper is an ex-Naval electrician with over 35 years of experience.

Can you replace an old fuse box?

Yes. Fusebox and panel upgrades, service change-outs, and grounding are all part of Blue Star's services, along with electrical power start-ups and permitting.

Do you patch the walls you open?

Yes. Sometimes it is safer to cut drywall than to risk damaging hidden wires. Blue Star handles the sheetrock patching when needed.
